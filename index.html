Frequently Asked Questions

Website banne mein kitna samay lagta hai?

Ek normal Business Website 3 se 5 din mein taiyaar ho jaati hai. E-Commerce Online Store mein products ke hisaab se 7 se 10 din lagte hain.

Kya mujhe website banane ke liye technical knowledge hona zaroori hai?

Bilkul nahi! Aapko sirf apne business ki jaankari aur photos deni hain. Design, domain, hosting aur Google setup sab hum karte hain.

Website banne ke baad agar mujhe koi photo ya details badalni ho toh?

Aap hamara Monthly Maintenance Plan (AMC) le sakte hain, ya jab bhi zaroorat ho chhote updates on-demand karwa sakte hain.

Kya website mobile phone par sahi dikhegi?

Haan, hamari banayi hui har website 100% mobile, tablet aur laptop responsive hoti hai.
